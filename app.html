neurology
Sentence Prediction model
Checking…
menu_bookSetup guide: enable WebGPU in Chromeexpand_more
  1. Open Chrome and type chrome://flags in the address bar. Websites can't open this page for you.
  2. Search for Vulkan and set it to Enabled
  3. Search for Unsafe WebGPU Support and set it to Enabled
  4. Tap Relaunch at the bottom of the flags page.
  5. Come back to Oryn, reopen the Sugarcane editor and switch Sentence Prediction on.
Needed mostly on Android and Linux. Without these flags the model still runs, but on the slower CPU path, so suggestions may lag or take longer to start. Pasting a copied link into the address bar jumps straight to that flag.
Runs fully on this device. Used by Sentence Prediction in the Sugarcane editor, which also needs AI Features and AI Usecases › Sugarcane turned on.
auto_awesome
Image Generation model
Checking…
A small, fun picture generator (512×512, low quality, no readable text). Runs fully on this device and adds a Generate tab to Insert Image in the Sugarcane editor. Needs AI Features and AI Usecases › Sugarcane turned on, and works best on a computer or a recent phone with plenty of free memory.
summarize
Summarization model
Checking…
Summarizes selected text or a whole document, fully on this device (SmolLM2-360M-Instruct). Adds a Summarize button to the Sugarcane editor. Needs AI Features and AI Usecases › Sugarcane turned on. Once downloaded it works fully offline on the CPU, with no browser flags needed. Long texts are read in parts, so they take longer.

The perks of having a
Oryn account
One tap with Google unlocks a smoother, safer, and more connected way to work with your documents.
password
No emails, no passwords
Skip the sign-up form entirely. Just continue with the Google account you already use — nothing new to create, nothing new to forget.
verified_user
No third-party servers, ever
We never ask you to trust a storage server you've never heard of. Your documents are saved straight to your own Google Drive — a place you already trust.
cloud_done
Your documents, everywhere
Start writing on your laptop, pick up right where you left off on your phone. Everything lives safely in the cloud and follows you, not the other way around.
folder_shared
Effortless sharing, both ways
Every document you create appears directly in your Google Drive, ready to share in seconds. And anything shared with you elsewhere in Drive shows up right inside Oryn automatically — no importing, no exporting.
workspace_premium
Exclusive roles & identity
Signed-in members can unlock special roles and a distinct identity badge inside the app — a small mark of being part of the Oryn community.
No hidden costs. No new accounts. Just your Google account, working harder for you.
