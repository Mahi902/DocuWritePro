## 5.8.0

* **Converters now work offline.** Every converter is saved on your device when you download the app, together with the libraries it needs. New converters added later are picked up automatically.
* **DyNote now works offline.** Its styling and fonts are saved on your device, so DyNote opens and works without internet.
* **Add-ons on the download screen.** After the offline download finishes you can browse the Add-ons Store and pick any add-ons to download right away. You can also skip it. Downloaded add-ons work offline too, including add-ons you add from a link or a file.
* **About App is now built into the app.** It follows your app theme, opens instantly and works offline, including the License, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Third Party Notices.
* Added Add-on support.
